Help & Support

What is TOURismo?

A Romblon tourism guide for discovering attractions, exploring photos, and finding directions.

How can I contact a destination?

Open a place and use its Facebook page or linked tourism office where available. Social-media searches are not official accounts.

Can I book or pay here?

No. TOURismo is an information and travel-planning guide, not a booking or payment service.

Are costs and opening hours guaranteed?

No. Check directly with local tourism offices or operators before travelling.
